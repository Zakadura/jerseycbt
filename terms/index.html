About this site

This website belongs to Rodrigo Silva (jerseycbt), a psychotherapist based in St Helier, Jersey. It's here to explain how I work, and to let you get in touch or book a consultation.

Not advice — and not an emergency service

The information here is general and isn't a substitute for individual professional advice or assessment. Nothing on this site, and nothing said in a free 15-minute call, is clinical treatment.

If you are in crisis or at risk of harm, please don't use this website to get help. Contact your GP, call 999, attend your nearest emergency department, or call the Samaritans free on 116 123, any time of day or night.

Booking, fees, and cancellations

Sessions are £100 for 50 minutes; the free 15-minute consultation is a fit call, not a session. Submitting the form or booking a call is a request — an appointment is confirmed once I reply to arrange it. I ask for at least 48 hours' notice to move or cancel a session; sessions cancelled with less notice may be charged.
