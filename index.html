I'm Rodrigo Silva, a BABCP and ACAT accredited cognitive behavioural psychotherapist based in St Helier, Jersey. With over 21 years of clinical experience across the NHS and private practice, I specialise in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) and Cognitive Analytical Therapy (CAT).

CBT therapy sessions are available in person at my St Helier office or online via secure video link — wherever you are in Jersey or the Channel Islands.
