Fees

Sessions are £100 for 50 minutes — the same whether we meet in Saint Helier or online. One clear rate: no packages, no tiers. The free 15-minute call beforehand is a fit conversation, not a session — a chance to say what you're bringing and hear how I'd work with it, with no obligation to book.
